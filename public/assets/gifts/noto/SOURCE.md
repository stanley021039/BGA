# Noto Emoji gift icons

190 unmodified 72px PNG files from [googlefonts/noto-emoji](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/tree/e20cbc2bbec1926686be9f9bee7d1d2cfa1fea0e/2D/png/72), commit `e20cbc2bbec1926686be9f9bee7d1d2cfa1fea0e`.

These icons are symbolic illustrations for original Afterhours gift titles. They do not depict a licensed commercial game's cards. Run `node scripts/fetch-gift-art.js` to fetch exactly the icons referenced by the built-in catalog from the pinned upstream revision.
